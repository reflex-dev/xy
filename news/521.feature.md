`xy.tooltip(format=...)` now formats dates and times. A strftime pattern —
`format={"time": "%b %d, %Y, %H:%M"}` — applies to any datetime-typed field
the tooltip can reach, including a column sourced from a data table (one bound
to a rendered channel), which previously ignored the format and rendered a raw
ISO stamp such as `2026-09-17 10:03:05.264Z`. The format is
resolved from either the author's column name or the channel it is bound to,
per hovered trace, so two series binding different columns to `y` keep their
own formats and the band title under `mode="x"` takes the format keyed by the
time column. A time value with no `format=` mostly stops falling back to
the ISO stamp as well: it follows the axis's own strftime `format=` when there
is one, and otherwise the pattern the visible span reads best in (`Sep 17,
2026` for a window of months, `Sep 17, 10:05` for hours or days, `10:05:00` for
seconds), sharpening as you zoom. Under a second the ISO stamp stays, being the
only form that carries milliseconds. `format=` also works on its own now, formatting the
default x/y/color/size rows without needing `fields=` or `title=`.
