The Reflex integration's chart data plane rides the app's own websocket as a
Reflex channel, with no socket.io anywhere. Binary columns now arrive beside
the message as frame attachments — aligned typed-array views over the received
frame rather than copies — and the per-message attachment budget rises from
socket.io-parser's hard limit of 10 to the channel frame's 64, so far more
figures ship their columns split instead of paying for a joined blob. The chart
component opens no connection of its own and mirrors none of Reflex's socket
options: it asks Reflex for the `/_xy` channel and rides whatever the app
already has.
