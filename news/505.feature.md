New `examples/bond` Reflex app: the 007 gun-barrel title sequence rendered as
one fixed-plan xy chart of six scatter layers, driven by a single
`@reflex_xy.data` var. The server publishes ~12 keyframes a second and
`xy.animation(match="index", update="interpolate")` renders the 60 fps motion in
between, with on-page controls for the publish rate, the point budget (up to
400k), and an interpolation on/off A/B.
