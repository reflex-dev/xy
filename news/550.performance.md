Static PNG export is faster whenever the plot area does not land on whole pixels, which is the default for many figure sizes and DPIs. The background behind the plot used to be blended one pixel at a time; now its interior is filled in one pass and only its one-pixel border is blended. Output is byte-identical. The matched 1800×840 line export against Matplotlib dropped from about 13 ms to about 8 ms, lifting the line family from about 8× to about 13× faster than Matplotlib/Agg.
