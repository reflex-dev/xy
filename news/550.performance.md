Static PNG export is faster when the plot area does not land on whole pixels, which is the default for many figure sizes and DPIs. The plot background used to be blended one pixel at a time. Now its interior is filled in one pass, and only its one-pixel border is blended. Output is byte-identical. In the matched 1800×840 `xy.pyplot` line export against Matplotlib/Agg, xy's total time fell by about 1.35–1.5×. The 200,000-sample line moved from about 7.4× to about 10.7× faster than Matplotlib, which clears the 10× target.
