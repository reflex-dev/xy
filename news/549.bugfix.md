`xy.pyplot` native mode now keeps calendar coordinates for pandas datetime-indexed `Series.plot()` / `DataFrame.plot()` under pandas 3.1, whose plotting paths pass raw period or business-day ordinals: daily, monthly, hourly and business-day indexes land on the native time axis in milliseconds since the epoch, while unrelated numeric overlays keep their units. pandas' minor period tickers are now ignored like its major ones, so PNG export no longer fails with `NotImplementedError`.
