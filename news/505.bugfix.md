`reflex_xy.setup(app)` is now idempotent per *app* rather than per process.
With more than one `rx.App` alive in a process — a hot reload, an AppHarness
test, two apps built side by side — setting up a second app used to leave the
first one's subscribers stranded (registry fan-out pointed only at the newest
channel), and setting the first one up again raised
`RuntimeError: A channel named '/_xy' is already registered`. Attachment is now
keyed on the app, and publishes, pushes and errors fan out to every attached
app. The app is held weakly throughout, so a superseded one is collected rather
than kept alive by the data plane.
