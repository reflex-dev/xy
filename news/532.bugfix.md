Fixed `xy.pyplot.fill_between` and `fill_betweenx` raising a `TypeError` when passed `datetime`, `datetime64`, or masked date sequences. Standalone datetime fills now render calendar tick labels without requiring a line overlay, and `fill_betweenx` strips with date extents keep their shape in PNG and SVG exports.
`set_xlim`/`set_ylim` (and `plt.xlim`/`plt.ylim`, `set_xbound`/`set_ybound`) now accept `datetime`, `date`, and `datetime64` limits, and date strings such as `"2026-01-02"` on a date axis.
Shared x axes also retain calendar ticks when only one panel contains dates and refresh after date fills are added or removed. Removing a split fill now removes every polygon, including masked and interpolated regions.
Date-axis inference no longer retains the original date container or changes when the caller later mutates it.
