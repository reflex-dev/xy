Added `xy.legend(loc="outside top")`, a legend band above the plot that never covers data: the plot shrinks to make room, and entries use as many columns as fit the width before wrapping into rows. It fixes the legend overlapping charts with no empty corner — stacked areas, many series, and bars that reach the axis maximum — where even `loc="best"` has nowhere to go. Matplotlib's `"outside upper center"` spelling is accepted too, and the browser and the SVG/PNG/PDF exports wrap the same entries into the same rows.
