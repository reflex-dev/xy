Long line and area charts decimate faster. When pixel columns average 8 or more rows, M4 now scans one column's rows at a time instead of updating a per-row state machine, with SIMD (the `argminmax` crate) for columns of 32 rows or more. Deep zooms keep the per-row loop. On one core, the decimation kernel is 3–9× faster at 1M points, the first payload of a 1M-point line chart is prepared 1.2–1.6× faster, and zooming into 10% of it re-decimates about 2× faster. The decimated rows do not change.
