Static PNG export is faster, most of all for contour charts. The rasterizer used to blend a plot background with fractional edges one pixel at a time, and it still drew Matplotlib's hidden default grid even though those lines were fully transparent. Now it fills the background interior with contiguous stores, skips transparent strokes and fills, and paints contour segment batches in parallel from a lower threshold. Marching squares also rejects cells that cross no level faster. Output pixels are unchanged. In the matched-output benchmark (1800×840 PNG, 4-vCPU VM), `xy.pyplot` contour export is 12.1× faster than Matplotlib/Agg on the 8-level smoke workload (was 7.2×) and 10.9× on the standard 12-level workload (was 6.7×). Line and histogram exports also now clear 10× on the standard profile.
